# Archived figures

## `bpe_mdl_curve.png`
Generated by `analysis/preferences/bpe_mdl.py`. Shows total description
length (corpus bits + vocab bits) vs BPE target vocabulary size V.

**Why archived:** MDL did not converge to an interior optimum in our
tested range (V ∈ [100, 500]); corpus-encoding cost dominates vocab-
encoding cost at our corpus size. Dashboard section 5.2 previously
reported this as a limitation; after 2026-04-23 we dropped MDL from the
V-selection narrative in favor of the compression-elbow heuristic +
robustness-across-V as the principled defense. The curve is preserved
for completeness but no longer referenced in the main write-up.

## `pair_feature_distributions.png`
Generated by `analysis/preferences/pair_features.py`. Shows distributions
of per-feature absolute differences for tied-outcome pairs.

**Why archived:** Superseded by the matched-pairs analysis
(`matched_pairs_volcano.png` and `matched_pairs_top_motifs.png` in
section 5.3) which does the same conditional-on-task comparison with
per-motif effect sizes and FDR control. The old aggregate plot is no
longer load-bearing.

## Phase A scaffolding (archived 2026-04-24)

See `output/paper2_pilot/_archive/phase_a_audit.md` (in the source
tree) for the compression decisions. The universals reframe makes the
BPE vocabulary the measuring instrument; Phase A 8-token DSL artifacts
are scaffolding that motivated the methodology and do not survive as
headline findings.

### `variance_decomposition.{png,csv,json}`
Generated by `analysis/preferences/variance_decomp.py`. Reports
sd(group_means)/sd(agent_means) per feature across three groupings
(task-id, fix-type, repo) on the 8-token DSL. Phase A scaffolding for
the dashboard's section 2.1; superseded at the BPE layer by per-motif
matched-pairs and per-agent JSD.

### `pair_action_levenshtein.png`, `pair_features.json`
Generated by `analysis/preferences/pair_features.py`. Pairwise
Levenshtein on tied-outcome action sequences over the 8-token DSL.
Phase A scaffolding for the dashboard's section 2.2; replaced by
`bpe_pair_levenshtein.png` and the matched-pairs analysis.

### `task_diversity_distribution.png`, `task_diversity_by_resolved.png`, `task_diversity.{csv,json}`
Generated by `analysis/preferences/task_diversity.py`. Per-task
procedural divergence across agents on the 8-token DSL. Phase A
scaffolding for the dashboard's section 2.3. Candidate to reclaim
